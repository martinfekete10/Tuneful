## Features

Tuneful brings native music controls for Spotify and Apple Music to your Mac. Choose the notch, menu bar, or mini player.

- **Notch player:** Hover over your MacBook's notch for quick music controls and satisfying haptic feedback.
- **Menu bar player:** See what's playing and use the built-in playback controls from a customizable popover.
- **Mini player:** Keep a compact player on screen and choose a style that suits you.
- **Favorites and integrations:** Save songs from any player, and connect Spotify or Last.fm for scrobbling and Now Playing updates.
- **Make it yours:** Customize the notch, menu bar, mini player, and keyboard shortcuts. Enable only the interfaces you want.
- **Native and lightweight:** Built with Swift and SwiftUI for a fast, smooth experience that uses minimal system resources.
